About 🧍‍♂️

Hi, I'm Ryan, the lead tutor at Studio Fluent. I hold a BA in Modern Languages (German and French) and have experience teaching both adults and children, from supporting students at university to my own tutoring practice.

Whatever your reasons for learning, my aim is to provide friendly, practical lessons that increase your confidence using your new language.

It is important that you like your course and your tutor. I am more than happy to arrange a call to discuss what you would like from the course and what I can offer.
